Most people don't lose money in the market because they lack information — they lose it because no one taught them how to read a chart, size a position, or sit on their hands.

This course exists to fix that. Every lesson is built from real, unfiltered NSE and BSE charts — not backtested fantasies. You'll learn how orders actually move price, how institutions build positions, and how to build a trading plan you can trust on your worst day, not just your best one.

No signals to copy. No "guaranteed returns." Just the mechanics of the market, taught the way a trading desk would teach a new analyst — and the discipline to use them.

Questions

No. Market Foundations starts from opening a Demat account and assumes nothing. If you already trade, you can start directly from Swing Mastery or Elite Mentorship.
