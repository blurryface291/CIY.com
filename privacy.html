1. Introduction

WeTraders Institute (“we”, “us”, “our”), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit https://ciyindia.com or enrol in our programmes, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have over it.

We are an educational institute. We deliberately collect as little personal data as possible — essentially only what we need to answer your enquiry, enrol you, take payment and teach you. We do not collect your trading account credentials, demat details, portfolio holdings or PAN-linked financial information, and we never ask you to share them.

This policy is written to meet the requirements of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, together with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it. A companion Data Protection Statement sets out our operational compliance measures in more detail.

6. Consent and withdrawal

Where we rely on consent, that consent is sought through a clear, standalone notice presented at or before the point of collection. It is free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, given by an affirmative action — we do not use pre-ticked boxes, and we do not bundle unrelated purposes into a single consent.

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it. You may withdraw at any time by:

  • emailing grievance@ciyindia.com with the subject line “Withdraw consent”;
  • replying STOP to any marketing message on WhatsApp; or
  • using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

Withdrawal takes effect prospectively. It does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and it does not require us to delete records we must retain by law (for example, invoices). If you withdraw consent that is necessary to deliver a programme you have enrolled in, we may no longer be able to provide that programme, and the Refund & Cancellation Policy will apply.

9. Storage and international transfer

Your data is stored on cloud infrastructure operated by our hosting and communications providers. Some of these providers operate servers outside India, which means your personal data may be transferred to and stored in another country.

Under the DPDP Act and the DPDP Rules, 2025, transfers outside India are permitted except to countries or territories that the Central Government may restrict by notification. We monitor any such notification and will change providers or storage regions if a restriction affects us. Wherever your data is stored, the protections described in this policy continue to apply.

11. How we protect data

We apply reasonable security safeguards appropriate to the limited and low-sensitivity data we hold, including: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS across the whole Website), access control on a need-to-know basis, strong and unique credentials with multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts, use of reputable managed service providers rather than self-hosted infrastructure, retention of security logs, prompt patching, and staff instruction on confidentiality.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please help us by keeping your access links and one-time passwords confidential and by contacting us at once if you suspect misuse.

12. Your rights

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access — obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we process it and with whom we have shared it;
  • Correction and completion — have inaccurate or misleading data corrected, and incomplete data completed or updated;
  • Erasure — have your personal data deleted where it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for and we are not required to keep it by law;
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, as easily as it was given;
  • Grievance redressal — a readily available means of raising a complaint with us; and
  • Nominate — nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any right, email grievance@ciyindia.com from the address registered with us, stating the right you wish to exercise. We may ask a question to verify your identity — we will not ask for a copy of any government ID unless it is strictly necessary. We will acknowledge within 48 hours and respond substantively within 30 days. There is no fee. You are required to provide only authentic information and not to impersonate another person when making a request.

13. Children's data

Our Services are intended for adults aged 18 and over, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The DPDP Act requires verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before processing a child's personal data, and prohibits tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children.

We do not carry out behavioural advertising at all. If you believe a person under 18 has provided us with personal data, contact grievance@ciyindia.com and we will delete it and cancel any associated enrolment. The same protections apply to a person with a disability who has a lawful guardian.

14. Data breaches

If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify each affected Data Principal without delay, in clear language, describing the nature and extent of the breach, the likely consequences, the measures we have taken and what you can do to protect yourself. We will also report the breach to the Data Protection Board of India within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and to CERT-In within the timelines applicable under its directions.

15. Third-party websites
